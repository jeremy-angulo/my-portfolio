Behind the scene

Thank you for visiting my portfolio!
If you are curious about the stack and how I built this project, here’s everything you need to know.

Three.js

Three.js is the library I’m using to render this 3D world.
It was created by mr.doob (X, GitHub), followed by hundreds of awesome developers, one of which being Sunag (X, GitHub) who added TSL, enabling the use of both WebGL and WebGPU, making this portfolio possible.

Open source

This world grew from the open-source folio of a mentor of mine (github.com/brunosimon/folio-2019), released under MIT license.
I rebuilt it into my own world — the career, the projects, the lab and a few corners of the island are mine. Thanks to him!

Musics

The music you hear was composed by the awesome Kounine (Linktree) and released under CC0 license.
